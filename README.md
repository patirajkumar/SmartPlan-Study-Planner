# **SmartPlan: Effortless Study Planner**

SmartPlan is an intuitive study planner designed to help students and professionals stay organized, manage tasks, and track progress effortlessly. With a user-friendly interface, SmartPlan allows users to plan their study schedule, set task priorities, and visualize their progress through interactive charts and a built-in calendar.

---

## **Features**

- **Task Management:** Add, edit, and delete tasks with details like name, subject, priority, and category (Homework, Project, Revision, Exam Prep).
- **Progress Tracker:** Visualize your completed and pending tasks with a real-time progress bar and pie chart.
- **Calendar Integration:** Stay on top of deadlines with a built-in calendar to view and organize tasks by date.
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized for both mobile and desktop devices, ensuring accessibility anywhere, anytime.

---

## **Technologies Used**

- **HTML5, CSS3** – For structure and styling.
- **JavaScript** – For interactivity and dynamic content.
- **FullCalendar** – For calendar functionality.
- **Chart.js** – For generating progress charts.
